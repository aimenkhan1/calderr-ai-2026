SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme Vendor Inc.
("Vendor") and Client Co. ("Client").

1. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement shall continue until terminated. Vendor may terminate this
Agreement at any time, for any reason, upon 5 days written notice. Client may
terminate only for material breach that remains uncured for 60 days after
written notice.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT
Client shall pay Vendor's invoices within 15 days of receipt. Late payments
accrue interest at 2% per month. Vendor may suspend all services immediately
upon any late payment without further notice.

3. DELIVERABLES
Vendor shall deliver all specified deliverables within 30 days of the
Effective Date. Time is of the essence. There is no cure period for missed
deliverable deadlines.

4. DATA HANDLING
Vendor may process Client's customer data as necessary to perform the
Services. Vendor may engage sub-processors without prior notice to Client.
Vendor will use commercially reasonable efforts to protect Client data.

5. LIABILITY
Each party's liability under this Agreement is capped at the total fees
paid in the preceding 12 months, except for breaches of confidentiality,
which are uncapped. Each party agrees to indemnify the other for third-party
claims arising from its own breach of this Agreement.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall keep the other's confidential information confidential
for a period of 3 years following disclosure.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
